ipmap Source

Where did your packets go?

Paste a traceroute and follow every hop around the globe. Each router's claimed location is checked against the speed of light, because a reply can't come from farther away than light travels in that time.

Run traceroute google.com on macOS or Linux, or tracert google.com on Windows, then paste everything it prints.

Runs entirely in your browser. To find locations, public hop addresses (and your own address, as the starting point) are looked up at ipwho.is, or ipapi.is if that's unavailable.
